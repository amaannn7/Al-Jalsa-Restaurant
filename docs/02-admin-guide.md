# Admin guide

Sign in at `/admin`. Every change is live on the website straight away; there is nothing to publish.

## Change a price
Menu items → click the dish → **Prices** → change the number (for example `42` or `42.50`) → **Save dish**.
Dishes with sizes have one row per size (for example Small / Large, Regular / Family Size). Leave a
price empty only if it is not confirmed; the site then shows "Price on request" and never totals it.

## Mark a dish as sold out for today
Menu items → click **Available** next to the dish (it turns grey). The site shows "Currently
unavailable" and the dish cannot be added to an order. Click again to bring it back.

## Add a new dish
Menu items → **Add dish** → fill the English name (Arabic is recommended), category, prices, and a
photo → **Add dish**.

### Photos
- **Photo:** a landscape (4:3) photo of the dish works best, at least 2000 px wide and under 4 MB.
  It is shown on the menu card, the home page and the dish details, and resized automatically.
- The current photos are representative stock photos (see `docs/photo-credits.md`). Replacing them
  with photos of the restaurant's own dishes is always an improvement.

## Featured and Popular
- **Featured** dishes appear in the home page carousel and a Featured tab on the menu.
- **Popular** adds a Popular tab. Only tick dishes you know are popular.

## Restaurant details
Phone, WhatsApp number (every order and enquiry goes here), address, Google Maps link, opening
hours (24-hour times; 06:00 to 01:00 means open past midnight), takeaway / delivery, delivery fee,
social links, Google rating. The "Still to confirm" ticks show reminders on the overview.

## Website text
English on the left, Arabic on the right, grouped by page. Empty Arabic falls back to English.

## Reviews
Paste genuine reviews exactly as written (including spelling), with the reviewer's name as shown
on Google. Do not edit or translate them.

## Enquiries
Every order, catering request, meeting request and message started on the site, with the full
WhatsApp text. Use **Reply on WhatsApp**, set the status (new / contacted / closed) and keep notes.
An entry here means the customer opened WhatsApp; it does not prove they pressed send.

## Admin users (owners only)
Create Editor accounts for staff. Editors can do everything except manage admin accounts and
delete enquiries. Deactivating an account signs it out everywhere.
